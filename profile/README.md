# ChargerDojo

The dojo where EV roaming integrations train before they meet production.

[chargerdojo.com](https://chargerdojo.com) is an OCPI testing platform: register a
connection to your endpoint, complete the credentials handshake, run real conformance
suites and timed end-to-end charging journeys, inspect every message exchanged, and
share the report with your partner. A free interactive simulator and learning hub
teach the protocol itself.

Speaks OCPI **2.1.1, 2.2, 2.2.1 and 2.3.0**, including the versions where megawatt
charging and payment terminals live.
